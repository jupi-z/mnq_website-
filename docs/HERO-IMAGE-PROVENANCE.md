# Homepage hero image provenance

`public/images/kolwezi-topography-light.webp` and `public/images/kolwezi-topography-dark.webp` are generated conceptual illustrations for the homepage background. Their palettes follow the official MonarQue Group logo; the logo itself is not embedded or altered.

They are not documentary photographs and do not depict a real Group site, project, mine, factory, or operation. The French and English captions on the homepage make this clear.
